About Semaglutide 关于司美格鲁肽（Semaglutide）

Semaglutide is a long-acting glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist originally developed by Novo Nordisk, Denmark. It simulates natural GLP-1 to provide dual benefits in glucose stabilization and weight management. 司美格鲁肽是一种长效的胰高血糖素样肽-1（GLP-1）受体激动剂，由丹麦诺和诺德公司研发。它能够模拟天然GLP-1激素，实现平稳降糖与控制体重的双重效果。

Mechanism of Action 核心作用机制

Glucose-dependent insulin secretion, glucagon suppression, delayed gastric emptying, and central appetite suppression. 葡萄糖浓度依赖性促进胰岛素分泌、抑制胰高血糖素释放、显著延缓胃排空及中枢性抑制食欲。

Indications 主要适应症

Adult Type 2 diabetes management (HbA1c reduction) and long-term weight management for obesity/overweight. 成年2型糖尿病血糖控制（降低HbA1c），以及肥胖或超重人群的长期体重管理。

Administration 给药方式

Available in clinical practice primarily as subcutaneous injection formulations (typically once weekly) and oral tablets. 临床上主要有皮下注射制剂（通常每周一次）及口服片剂。

Safety Notice 安全提示

May cause mild-to-moderate gastrointestinal reactions. Strict medical supervision required; contraindicated in MTC history. 常见轻中度胃肠道反应。需遵医嘱使用，有甲状腺髓样癌病史者禁用。
